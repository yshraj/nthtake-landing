# Landing page content

Nthtake waitlist page. One studio link to review, revise, and unlock the master.

## Page flow

1. **Intro** - short loader on first visit
2. **Nav** - How, Takes, Questions, Get early access
3. **Hero** - "Less back-and-forth. Clearer feedback." First-name waitlist
4. **Tools** - Premiere, After Effects, DaVinci, Figma, Photoshop, Final Cut
5. **Stakes** - ghosting after delivery, 3,200 freelancers
6. **Unlock demo** - watermarked preview, hold to unlock the master
7. **How** - Lock brief, Cut version, Client reviews, One round, Iterate, Approve
8. **Takes** - before / after version compare
9. **Updates** - send the take, not a recap
10. **Review** - numbered notes, submit this round
11. **FAQ** - payment gate, screen-record, one round, old takes, minor monthly fees
12. **Closer** - email waitlist + minor monthly fees
13. **Footer** - product, legal, company

## Waitlist

- Hero: first name
- Closer: work email + craft (video / design / other)
- No subscription. Minor monthly fees.
